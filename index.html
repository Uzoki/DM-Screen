    Carrying & Encumbrance

    A Small or Medium creature can carry 15 × its Strength score in pounds, and can drag, push, or lift twice that amount. Each size category above Medium doubles both totals; a Tiny creature halves them.

    Carrying Capacity

    Encumbrance (Optional Rule)

    Carrying more than 5 × Strength imposes the Encumbered condition (Speed −10 feet). Carrying more than 10 × Strength, up to the maximum carrying capacity, imposes Heavily Encumbered instead (Speed −20 feet, plus Disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws).
